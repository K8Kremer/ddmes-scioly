Background

New to Science Olympiad?

Our team is open to students in grades 3–5. We compete in Division A, the elementary division, and follow the North Carolina Science Olympiad rules. Students pair up to specialize in 2-3 events and prepare for them over the course of the year. Some events are written: identifying rocks, stars, or specimens. Some are built: bridges, launchers, design challenges. The regional tournament runs in one morning, and every student's score feeds the team result.

The part that surprises most parents: it isn't a science-fair-style contest where one kid wins. NCSO compares it to a track meet, which is exactly right. Students specialize in their events and their scores combine to contribute to the team's overall score.

The current slate of events and the full rules manual are posted at NC Science Olympiad.
