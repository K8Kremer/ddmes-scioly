How Fridays work

We meet Fridays, 4:15–5:00 PM, at school. Each Friday one event group meets, rotating through four groups. Your student's regular meetings are the Fridays for the groups their events are in. Please try not to miss those.

  • Field AGeology Rocks!, Buzzworthy, Codebusters
  • Field BDeep Blue Sea, Roots & Reptiles, Storm Chasers
  • Diagram & DecodeDescribe It Build It, Pump It Up, Science Sketchers, Zap Lab
  • Build & TestBeam Me Up, Just Plane Awesome, Ship Shape

Other Fridays are optional. Students are welcome on Fridays when their group isn't meeting. They work at the Open Lab table on an independent lab for one of their events or a related science activity. Coming or skipping are both fine; just let your student's teacher know the pickup plan.

Bring the SciOly binder to every Friday your student attends. It goes home in between.

Bridge-A-Roni and Egg-O-Naut don't meet on Fridays. Those event families set their own build times.

When a Friday is cancelled, the rotation picks up where it left off the next week, so every group keeps its turns.
