Practice at home, by event

Everything here is the same material that's in your student's binder, so you can print a fresh copy if pages go missing or look ahead together. Jump to the events your student is doing:

  • Field AGeology Rocks!, Buzzworthy, Codebusters
  • Field BDeep Blue Sea, Roots & Reptiles, Storm Chasers
  • Diagram & DecodeDescribe It, Build It, Pump It Up, Science Sketchers, Zap Lab
  • Build & TestBeam Me Up, Just Plane Awesome, Ship Shape
  • Saturday buildsBridge-A-Roni, Egg-O-Naut

For each event there's a lesson guide (what we cover at each meeting, plus at-home practice) and the event's journal (Field Journal, Case File, or Engineer's Logbook).

Pages are set up for double-sided printing. The official rules and event descriptions are at NC Science Olympiad.
